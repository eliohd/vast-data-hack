## What changed (Manhattan demo polish)

- Fixture: `web/src/lib/rideahead/canonical-snapshots.json` is **generated by `make gen-api`** (A's
  `app.export_fixtures`). B never hand-edits or recopies it. Offline chapters come from its frames
  (`previewChapters()`); new tests derive expectations from its structure.
- Map: location-agnostic Web Mercator, north up (`lib/rideahead/projection.ts`). The London-tuned
  `rotate(-60)` and fixed box are gone; `RouteMap.tsx` (unused) removed.
- Hazard markers never hidden: every overlay carries `data-map-chrome`; `useMapChrome` measures them and
  `mapFit.ts` fits routes, riders and markers into the largest unobstructed rectangle. Road view sits
  under the decision card, has a minimise/expand button and auto-collapses if no free area is big enough.
- Road view (`roadView.ts`): when the current observation has a playable recorded `media_ref`, the clip
  auto-plays (muted, playsInline, `#t=start,end`) labelled exactly **"Recorded footage · cached Cosmos
  check"** with the Cosmos verdict, rationale and the claim's trust label. Autoplay rejection shows "Tap
  to play recorded clip"; load errors show "Clip failed to load". Media only via `/api/archive/media` +
  demo key. Synthetic observations render no `<video>`, only a striped "SYNTHETIC" card.
- Trust layer (`trust.ts`): each claim is "Claim rejected by verification" (server `support` or verifier
  verdict contradicted, still playable), "Claim supported by verification", "Verification inconclusive"
  or a synthetic label. The evidence panel lists all revealed claims under "Verification layer".
- Synthetic labels: SYNTHETIC callout on map markers, "SYNTHETIC EVENT" badge on the decision card when
  the decision's evidence is synthetic, "Synthetic event/clearance · not recorded footage" in Road view,
  evidence panel and the decision event line.
- Decision change (`decisionChange.ts`): when `decision.changed` (with a previous decision in
  `decision_history`), the card flashes ~1.6 s (static outline under reduced motion), shows a "Route
  changed"/"Recommendation changed" badge and an event line (`t=2:40 Recommendation changed: A → B`);
  the Timeline panel keeps a "Decision changes" log. Fired once per `decision_id:revision`.
  `detour_cost_s` ("+39 s detour") and `arrival_in_s` ("Report ahead in ~1 min 59 s") show when present.
- Lifecycle styles (`lifecycle.ts`): one mapping for markers, legend and chips; each state has its own
  colour, icon and outline (stale = faded amber, dashed, clock; cleared = green, solid, check).
- Radius sliders: Apply calls `PATCH /api/rideahead/sessions/{session_id}/config` via the typed client
  (unit-tested). User actions now queue behind in-flight polls instead of being dropped; failures show an
  inline alert and keep the draft.
- Camera: `getUserMedia` only runs after tapping "Use camera" (verified, no call on load).

## Known limitations

- No street basemap. Antimeridian-crossing routes are not handled.
- `arrival_in_s` is "seconds until the hazard" per the schema, so it is labelled "Report ahead in ~…",
  not destination arrival.
- "Cosmos" wording assumes Cosmos is the verifier for `cached_model`/`live_model`; `fixture` source is
  labelled "fixture check · no Cosmos call".
